2. UNDERSTANDING CATEGORIES VS. SERVICES
-------------------------------------------------------
This is the most important part! Imagine your website is a big grocery store.
- "Home Page Service Categories" are the BIG AISLE SIGNS (like "Fruit Aisle", "Dairy Aisle").
- "Services" are the ACTUAL ITEMS on the shelves (like "Apples", "Milk").
- "Dropdown Tags" are the little sticky notes you put on an item so you know which aisle to put it in!

Here is the exact order to add a brand new service to your website:

STEP 1: Create the Sticky Note (Dropdown Tag)
1. On the left side menu, click "Categories" (under Configuration).
2. Look at the top box that says "Service Categories".
3. In the "New service category..." box, type a name (Example: "Winter Specials").
4. Click the "+ Add" button.

STEP 2: Create the Big Aisle Sign (Home Page Section)
1. On the left side menu, click "Service Categories" (under Management).
2. Click the "+ Add Category" button at the top right.
3. Type the EXACT SAME NAME you used in Step 1 for the Title (Example: "Winter Specials").
4. Write a small description for your customers to read.
5. Make sure the "Visible on website" box is checked.
6. Click "Save Category".
*Now your website has a brand new section on the home page!*

STEP 3: Add the Actual Service (The Item on the Shelf)
1. On the left side menu, click "Services" (under Workspace).
2. Click the "+ Add Service" button at the top right.
3. Fill in the Name (Example: "Snow Salt Removal").
4. Pick the Category from the dropdown menu (Pick "Winter Specials").
5. Type in the Price.
6. Upload a nice picture of a clean car.
7. Click "Save Service".
*Boom! The service will magically appear inside the new section on your live website!*

-------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO READ CUSTOMER QUOTES
-------------------------------------------------------
1. On the left side menu, click "Submissions".
2. Here you will see a list of everyone who asked for a quote!
3. Click on any customer's name to see what car they have, what they need, and any pictures they sent you.
4. When you are done reading it, you can click the red trash can to delete it so your list stays clean.

-------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO ADD CUSTOMER REVIEWS
-------------------------------------------------------
1. On the left side menu, click "Testimonials".
2. Click "+ Add Review".
3. Type the customer's name (Example: "John D.").
4. Pick how many stars they gave you (5 is the best!).
5. Copy and paste what they wrote in the "Comment" box.
6. Click "Save". It will show up on the website instantly!

-------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW TO ADD PICTURES TO THE GALLERY
-------------------------------------------------------
1. On the left side menu, click "Gallery".
2. You can drag and drop your awesome before-and-after pictures right into the dotted box.
3. Click "Upload". Your website gallery is now updated!

-------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY CHECKLIST:
-------------------------------------------------------
If a service is NOT showing up on your website, ask yourself these 3 questions:
[ ] Did I create the Dropdown Tag in the "Categories" tab?
[ ] Did I create the Big Aisle Sign in the "Service Categories" tab?
[ ] Does the Aisle Sign have the EXACT same title as the Dropdown Tag?

If all 3 are YES, your service will always show up perfectly!
